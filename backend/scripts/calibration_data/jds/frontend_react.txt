Senior Frontend Engineer (React)
Openfield Learning, Manchester (hybrid)

About us
Openfield builds an online learning platform used by 400 schools and colleges. Our learners range from 11-year-olds to adults returning to education, so accessibility and clarity are not optional extras for us.

The role
You will join the Learner Experience team to build the screens learners use every day: lessons, quizzes, progress and feedback. You will shape our component library and help the wider team raise the bar on accessibility and performance.

What you'll do
- Build features in React and TypeScript, from data fetching to polished, responsive interfaces.
- Grow and maintain our design system in Storybook, working closely with product designers in Figma.
- Make sure everything we build meets WCAG 2.2 AA and works well with keyboards and screen readers.
- Improve page load and interaction performance on low-end devices and slow school networks.
- Write component and end-to-end tests that give the team confidence to ship daily.

What you'll need
- Strong experience with React and TypeScript in production applications.
- A deep understanding of HTML, CSS and accessibility, including ARIA and screen reader testing.
- Experience building or maintaining a component library or design system.
- Experience with testing tools such as Jest or Vitest, React Testing Library and Playwright or Cypress.
- Knowledge of web performance: bundle size, Core Web Vitals and profiling.
- Experience with a data-fetching library such as TanStack Query or Redux.

Nice to have
- Next.js or server-side rendering experience.
- Experience with animation libraries or motion design.
- An interest in education.

Benefits
- Salary 65,000 to 78,000 pounds.
- Four-day week option, private healthcare and a home-office budget.
