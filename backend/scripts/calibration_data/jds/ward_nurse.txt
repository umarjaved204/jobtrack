Staff Nurse, Acute Medicine (Band 5)
Riverside Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust, Derby

About us
Riverside Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust provides acute and community services to 600,000 people across Derbyshire. Our values are kindness, respect and teamwork.

The role
We are looking for registered nurses to join our acute medical wards. You will care for adults admitted with a wide range of acute illnesses, working as part of a supportive multidisciplinary team. Full-time and part-time posts are available, with internal rotation opportunities.

Main duties
- Assess, plan, deliver and evaluate person-centred care for a group of acutely unwell patients.
- Recognise and escalate deteriorating patients, using the National Early Warning Score.
- Administer medicines safely, including intravenous medicines, in line with trust policy.
- Work closely with doctors, therapists and social care colleagues to plan safe discharges.
- Support, supervise and assess nursing students and healthcare assistants.
- Take charge of the ward in the absence of the ward manager when competent to do so.

Essential criteria
- Registered nurse (adult) with current NMC registration.
- Experience in an acute hospital setting.
- Good communication skills with patients, families and colleagues.
- Commitment to ongoing professional development.

Desirable criteria
- IV therapy and cannulation competencies.
- Immediate Life Support certificate.
- Mentorship or practice assessor training.

What we offer
- Band 5 salary on the NHS Agenda for Change pay scale.
- NHS pension, 27 days of annual leave rising with service, and a preceptorship programme.
