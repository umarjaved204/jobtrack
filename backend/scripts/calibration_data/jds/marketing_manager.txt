Content Marketing Manager
Ledgerly, London (hybrid)

About us
Ledgerly makes bookkeeping software for accountants and their small business clients. We are growing fast and want our content to become the place accountants go to learn.

The role
You will own our content programme from strategy to publication: blog posts, guides, webinars, newsletters and customer stories. You will work with product marketing, sales and our in-house designer to bring in qualified leads and support customers.

What you'll do
- Set the content strategy and editorial calendar, based on research into what accountants search for and ask about.
- Write and edit long-form content, landing pages and email campaigns.
- Grow organic traffic through SEO, working with our web team on technical improvements.
- Run our newsletter and nurture campaigns in HubSpot.
- Measure performance with Google Analytics and report on leads and pipeline.
- Manage freelance writers and agencies.

Requirements
- Five or more years of experience in content marketing, ideally in B2B software.
- Excellent writing and editing skills, with a portfolio to show us.
- Strong SEO knowledge.
- Experience with HubSpot or a similar marketing automation platform.
- Experience reporting with Google Analytics.
- Experience managing writers, freelancers or a small team.

Nice to have
- Experience with webinars or podcast production.
- Knowledge of accounting or finance.

Benefits
- Salary 55,000 to 65,000 pounds.
- 30 days of holiday, enhanced parental leave and a learning budget.
